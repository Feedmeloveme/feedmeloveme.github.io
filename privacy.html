Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Android game Feed Me & Love Me (package com.feedmeloveme.game) and this website handle information.

Effective date November 17, 2026
Developer Zemvuu
Privacy contact feedmeloveme@proton.me

Playing and saving locally

You can play without an account. The game saves animal names, care, progress, diamonds, achievements, memories and preferences on your device. The app can display care reminders with the chosen name of a companion if you enable reminders and grant Android notification permission. Clear the app’s data in Android Settings to remove the local save and preferences. Uninstalling also removes the app’s local storage. Cloud saves are managed separately.

Google sign-in and cloud backup

If you choose to connect a Google account, Android’s Google sign-in and Firebase Authentication verify your account. Google may provide account information such as your Google user identifier, email address and profile information to Firebase Authentication. Firestore stores a backup under your Firebase user ID. The backup includes the pet names you enter, progress and preferences. Please avoid using a real person’s name or other personal information as a pet name. Cloud backup is optional and carries progress across devices. While you play, a changed save can be uploaded at most once every 72 hours after the first successful backup. Backups are kept with your cloud account until deletion. Access is restricted to the authenticated account that owns the save.

Optional game statistics

Firebase Analytics is off until you enable Optional game statistics in Settings. When enabled, it measures launches and engagement, screens, adoption, care, achievements and rewarded ads. Analytics may process app-instance identifiers, device or app information, approximate location inferred from IP and interaction data. We exclude pet names, email addresses and Firebase sign-in IDs from the events we send. The app disables Analytics advertising ID collection and its advertising-related consent signals. You can switch statistics off in Settings to stop future collection. Data previously processed is subject to the configured retention period and Google’s applicable policies. You can contact us about access or deletion.

Advertising and choices

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display an adaptive banner and offer optional rewarded ads. The SDK may collect and share IP address, device or account identifiers, interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. These signals may include approximate location. Google’s User Messaging Platform checks whether an advertising privacy message is needed and records your choices. If the advertising settings for your region provide a privacy form, you can reopen it in Settings under Ad privacy choices. These choices are separate from the game statistics switch. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Delete your cloud account and backup

In the app, open Settings, choose Delete cloud account and confirm with the connected Google account. This removes the Firebase account and the associated Firestore save. The local game remains on the device. To remove it as well, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings. This procedure does not delete your Google account.

If you no longer have the app, send a deletion request to feedmeloveme@proton.me and include the email address of the Google account used to sign in. We verify ownership before processing the request. Never send a password or a verification code. You can also ask us about deletion of other data and any applicable retention exceptions.

Use of data and your rights

We use the data necessary to run the game features you request, including optional sign-in and backup. We rely on consent for optional game statistics and request advertising consent through Google’s form where required. Google may also process data for security and other purposes under its own policies. Network connections to the services use encryption in transit. Google and its providers may process data outside your country. See Firebase Privacy and Security for details.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive your data, restrict or object to some processing, withdraw consent and complain to a data protection authority. To exercise your rights or ask about retention or international transfers, contact feedmeloveme@proton.me.

Children and this website

The game includes virtual animal neglect, loss and remembrance. If you have concerns about a child’s data, contact us. This static website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub may process network and usage data as its hosting provider. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes and contact

We will post changes to this policy here with an updated effective date. Contact Zemvuu at feedmeloveme@proton.me.
