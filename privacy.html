Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Android game Feed Me & Love Me (package com.feedmeloveme.game) and this website handle information.

Effective date: November 2, 2026
Developer: Zemvuu
Privacy contact: feedmeloveme@proton.me

1. The game and local information

The game does not require creating a user account or providing a real name. It saves gameplay information on your device, such as animals and their names, care status, days together, gems, achievements, themes, language, and notification settings. We do not intentionally transmit the names you choose for your animals as Firebase analytics event parameters.

The app enables Android backup. Depending on your device and account settings, your data may be copied to backup services controlled by Android and Google. Removing the app does not necessarily delete older device backups.

2. Advertising

The closed-test app integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK and currently uses Google's official test ad identifiers. The SDK may process information such as IP address (and inferred approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic information. Test ads do not guarantee zero data processing by Google. The app uses optional rewarded video ads to award in-game currency. Real-money purchases are not enabled in the present closed test.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads privacy documentation.

3. Optional analytics and diagnostics

The app integrates Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring. Their data collection is disabled by default in the current version and turned on only with your optional diagnostics consent. You may change this choice in the app settings. When enabled, Analytics may record limited game events such as animal adoption, feeding, achievements and rewarded ads, as well as device or app usage information. Crashlytics may process crash reports and technical device data, and Performance Monitoring may record app performance metrics.

Turning off optional analytics or diagnostics prevents future collection by our app configuration but does not immediately delete information already processed by Google. See Firebase Privacy and Security for details.

4. Notifications and permissions

When you enable care reminders, the game may ask for permission to send Android notifications. Reminders may contain the locally stored name of a companion. Reminder preferences and animal names are kept in the app's local storage for notifications. The app also uses internet and network-state access for connected services and ads. It does not request precise location, contacts, microphone or camera permission in the current version.

5. Purposes and third parties

We use local information to provide gameplay and maintain your progress. With your consent, diagnostics and analytics help us understand engagement and fix errors. Advertising services may use data for delivering and measuring advertisements, security and fraud prevention, depending on their settings and applicable laws. We do not sell your locally stored animal names as a dataset. Google processes information through its SDKs; its role and use of data depend on the service.

6. EEA and UK choices

For users covered by European privacy laws, optional Firebase analytics and diagnostics are operated on the basis of consent. You can withdraw your consent in app settings. Strictly necessary game storage supports the game you requested. Advertising-related processing may require additional consent or other lawful grounds; the developer must implement any required advertising consent controls before using production ads. This policy does not itself constitute consent.

7. Retention and deletion

Local progress generally remains on your device until you reset it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Some settings and device backups may remain after an in-game reset. You can delete local data using Android Settings → Apps → Feed Me & Love Me → Storage → Clear data; you may also need to manage Android backups separately.

Retention of information processed by Google advertising or Firebase services depends on Google's specific policies and configurations. We cannot guarantee that clearing app data immediately deletes information already received by Google. For inquiries about data or deletion options, contact feedmeloveme@proton.me. Do not send passwords or other sensitive personal data.

8. Security and international processing

We rely on Android platform security measures and Google SDKs that use encrypted network connections where applicable. No technology is entirely risk-free. Some Google services or their subprocessors may process data in other countries under their applicable safeguards and policies.

9. Children

The game is not specifically intended for young children. Its themes include virtual animal neglect, loss and remembrance. We do not intentionally ask children to register accounts or supply their real names. Parents and guardians should assess whether the content is suitable. If you have concerns about a child's data, contact us.

10. This website

This is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. We have not included website analytics scripts, advertising scripts, sign-in forms or nonessential cookies. GitHub may process visitors' network and usage data as the hosting provider. See GitHub's privacy statement. Links to external services are covered by their own privacy notices.

11. Changes

We may revise this policy if the app or its data practices change, including when Google Play Billing or production advertising is introduced. Updates will be published on this page with a new effective date, and consent will be requested again where required.
